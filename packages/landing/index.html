Three sealed bids. Only the winner is opened.

Each bid on Zcash testnet was a shielded deposit of exactly 0.02 TAZ, its memo a commitment to the bid. A Groth16 proof then showed which commitment holds the highest bid, so the losing bids are never published, not even in the result.

What happened on testnet

  1. Three valid sealed bids, plus four deposits the auction refused: a wrong amount, a memo that is not a bid, a duplicate commitment, and one after the close.
  2. A bidder tried to reveal a different bid with its real salt. Refused: the opening matches no commitment.
  3. One shielded settlement (89bf60fd…) paid the seller 0.0175 TAZ and refunded everyone else. Each recipient decrypted only its own outputs with its own viewing key.

What you still trust

The auctioneer sees bids when they are revealed and is trusted to settle. Zcash has no contracts, so the rules run in the auction service, but every input to them can be re-read from the chain with the published incoming viewing key. The proof uses a single-party Groth16 setup.
