Frequently asked questions

What Fuin hides, and what you still trust.

Who ever sees my bid?

Only the auctioneer, when bids are revealed to it after the close. Losing bids appear nowhere public: not in the proof, on the chain or on the results page.

What do I still trust?

The auctioneer settles. Zcash has no contracts, so the rules run in the auction service, but every input to them can be re-read from the chain with the published incoming viewing key.

How is the winner proven?

A Groth16 MaxBid proof shows the declared winner holds the highest committed bid at or above the reserve. Its public inputs are exactly the deposit memos on the chain. The setup is single-party.

Is it live?

On Zcash testnet, with every transaction linked above. Not audited.
